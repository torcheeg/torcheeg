TorchEEG adapts the spatial encoder and summary-token architecture, retains
encoder tensor names, uses TorchEEG constructor naming and documentation,
adds explicit electrode IDs and validated mask selection, repairs temporal
selection geometry, and validates complete local tensor checkpoint states.
The original workspace's normalized-patch option is preserved separately.
The task predictor/reconstructor and downstream heads are separate scope;
this encoder does not claim to implement their pretraining objectives.
