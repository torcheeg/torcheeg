LEAD optional external backend
=============================

No LEAD author implementation or official checkpoint is bundled with this
TorchEEG adapter. The caller supplies a trusted, separately obtained local
backend and checkpoint. The adapter code is part of TorchEEG; it does not
change the terms applicable to those external materials.
