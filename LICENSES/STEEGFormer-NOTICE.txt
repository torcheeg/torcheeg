The EEG patch and positional embeddings and encoder tensor names follow
pretrain/models_mae_eeg.py. The fixed 142-name channel/index table was decoded
from literal entries in pretrain/senloc_file/sen_chan_idx.pkl without executing
pickle globals. The learned table retains all 145 native slots.

The encoder uses registered PyTorch modules and native scaled-dot-product
attention, with no timm or torchvision dependency. Feature methods explicitly
separate native pretraining final normalization from downstream unnormalized
mean pooling. The MAE decoder, optimizer and task-specific weights are not
implemented or represented as restored by an encoder load.

No pretrained weights are redistributed. The small native release artifact
is linked from the author's repository. Its SHA256 and release provenance
are recorded in the model guide; checkpoint deserialization remains safe and
explicit about argparse.Namespace metadata and discarded decoder components.


Downstream mean pooling and linear-head semantics follow
easy_start/models_vit_eeg.py at the same revision. TorchEEG wrappers expose
explicit whole-window resampling, optional signal normalization, task-head
dropout, channel-major features and freeze/evaluation control. They initialize
fresh task heads and do not claim to restore native trained task weights.
Independent fixtures use deterministic synthetic weights and signals.
